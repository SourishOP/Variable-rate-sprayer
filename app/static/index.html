How PaddyGuard works

A rice leaf image goes down two parallel paths: a deep-learning classifier names the disease, while image segmentation measures how much of the leaf is diseased. Those combine into a severity level that drives the variable-rate spray decision.

1

Classify disease

EfficientNetV2-B0 CNN predicts the disease class (or Healthy) with a confidence score. Low-confidence guesses are flagged.

2

Measure severity

A trained lesion segmenter marks diseased pixels; severity = diseased ÷ leaf area. Trace the leaf for precise isolation.

3

Map to level

Severity → Healthy / Low / Medium / High, giving a base coefficient. The disease type then scales the dose.

4

Variable-rate spray

The effective coefficient sets the solenoid open time (a real ~0.5–3s valve window). Healthy leaves get no spray.

PaddyGuard · EfficientNetV2-B0 + segmentation severity · based on Tewari et al. (2020) methodology
